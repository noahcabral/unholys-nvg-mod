The adaptation reorients and assembles the original bridge and both solid housings, moves the paired housings inward, adds four fitted optical surfaces and assigns opaque black materials to the original interior wells, assigns game materials, and rigs the assembly to Romeo's G24 receiver. The existing G24 mount, shaders, and textures retain their respective project terms.
