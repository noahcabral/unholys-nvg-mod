# Asset credits and distribution

## GPNVG-18

The quad housing adapts **Modern NVG/GPNVG18 Dummy [Weight Optimized/somewhat rugged]** by FaboElChapo, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The adaptation reorients the bridge and housings, adds fitted optical surfaces and in-game materials, and rigs the assembly. The original housing and its adaptation retain those terms. The shared G24 assembly has its own provenance. [Original model](https://www.printables.com/model/1097091-modern-nvggpnvg18-dummy-weight-optimizedsomewhat-r/files) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

## Other model inputs

The in-game ALPHA and Mono housings adapt the Vonscherf PVS-69 designs from the supplied `ALPHA FDM v82.3mf` and `Mono v8.3mf`. The supplied `wilcox-g24-1.snapshot.1.zip` provides the G24 mount geometry. The supplied `improved_OBJ_lens_8mm_m16.stl` provides the objective-lens geometry. The mod's owner confirmed noncommercial redistribution terms for these inputs. The bundled game meshes and textures are offered for nonprofit, noncommercial use. Retain these credits when redistributing the model assets. The GPNVG-18 adaptation also retains its CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms. [PVS-69 project](https://github.com/knack-tactical/PVS-69)

## Engine, shaders and screenshots

The Romeo download includes the Screen Space Shaders glass vertex shader by Ascii1457, adapted 3D Shader Scopes shaders, and Romeo PiP postprocessing files. The screenshots are from the Romeo build of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly.
