# Asset credits and distribution

## GPNVG-18

The quad housing adapts **Modern NVG/GPNVG18 Dummy [Weight Optimized/somewhat rugged]** by FaboElChapo, under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The adaptation reorients the bridge and housings, adds fitted optical surfaces and in-game materials, and rigs the assembly. The original housing and its adaptation retain those terms. The shared G24 assembly has its own provenance. [Original model](https://www.printables.com/model/1097091-modern-nvggpnvg18-dummy-weight-optimizedsomewhat-r/files) · [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

## Other model inputs

The supplied ALPHA and Mono 3MF files had a title entry with no distribution terms. The supplied G24 STEP archive and separate objective-lens input also need explicit public redistribution provenance. Those inputs are incorporated into the exported in-game geometry, including the G24 on the quad. The local builder keeps that compiled geometry and its texture set in a separate input.

## Engine, shaders and screenshots

The repo carries the authored Lua, item data, shader modules and donor patches. The Screen Space Shaders glass vertex shader by Ascii1457, the 3D Shader Scopes shader donors and the exact PiP postprocess donor files are taken from the player's installation during a local build. The screenshots are from the Romeo build of S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly. [Screen Space Shaders](https://www.moddb.com/mods/stalker-anomaly/addons/screen-space-shaders) · [GSC Game World fan content guidelines](https://www.gsc-game.com/guidelines/)
